- Read answer acceptance and topology behavior. Miners submit candidate sets governed by optional minimum valid answer count, energy threshold, and diversity floor; mirror-symmetric spin answers are considered equivalent. - Continued native/job lifecycle. Native jobs accept arbitrary coupling graphs but cannot be solved by miners requiring hardware embedding (e.g. QPU); testnet simulated-annealing miners can. `solve()` posts a bid/reward to escrow, miners across CPU/GPU/quantum-annealing may compete (unless restricted), the chain rechecks spins/energy/diversity/quality, and miners cannot grade themselves.
